Stash Style guide
Stash · Visual direction · v0.1

Three ways Stash could look.

Every option shares the same type, spacing, motion and components. Only colour and the mascot's paint change. Pick one in the header; the whole page, including the landing page, canvas and chat below, repaints. Each option has a light and a dark mode.

01 · Mascot

Meet Stash.

A storage box with its lid lifted just enough to peek out. Stash keeps an eye on everything you save, so its eyes do the talking: they follow you, scan while searching, smile when something is found, and flatten when it won't help. It replaces the sparkle icon that every AI feature uses.

Move your pointer. Stash is watching.
Idle

Default. Blinks every few seconds. Chat button, header.

Searching

Lid up, eyes scan. While chat retrieves cards.

Found

Smiling eyes. Answer with citations arrived.

Checking

Squinting. Image or card waiting on the safety check.

Won't help

Lid shut, flat eyes. Blocked question or content.

Resting

Eyes closed. Empty states and nothing found.

Sizes · built on a 64-unit grid, 2.5-unit outline
Mascot rules
  • Use Stash wherever the AI acts: chat, search status, safety checks.
  • Its state matches what the system is doing. It never pretends to work.
  • Eye-following only on the landing page and the empty canvas, where it is a welcome, not a distraction.
  • No speech bubbles, jokes or personality copy. Stash shows, the product speaks plainly.
  • No bouncing, confetti or looping idle dances. Blinks only.
  • Never recolour beyond the option's three mascot tokens.
02 · Colour

One accent per view.

Neutrals carry the interface. The accent is reserved for the primary action, focus, selection and chat highlights. No gradients, no glow. Values update with the option and mode you pick.

03 · Typography

Geist for words, Geist Mono for data.

Two families, five sizes in the product. Headings balance, body text is capped near 65 characters, numbers use tabular figures. Only display sizes tighten their tracking.

05 · Icons

Hugeicons, stroke 1.5, 24 grid.

One icon family across the product (@hugeicons/react, stroke style). Brand marks for Google and X come from their official SVGs (thesvg.org). Icon-only buttons always carry an accessible label and a tooltip.

shadcn/ui, restyled with these tokens.

Radix primitives underneath for focus and keyboard behaviour; shadcn's structure; Stash's tokens on top. Destructive actions always confirm in a dialog. Errors appear next to the field that caused them.

def verify(body, sig, secret):
    mac = hmac.new(secret, body,
                   hashlib.sha256)
    # constant-time compare
    return hmac.compare_digest(
        mac.hexdigest(), sig)

10 · Quality bar

What keeps it from looking generated.

Drawn from the ui-skills baseline and Emil Kowalski's design-engineering rules, adapted to Stash.

Do
  • One accent per view. Neutrals do the rest.
  • Real copy everywhere: real card titles, real counts, real errors that say how to fix them.
  • Every empty state has exactly one next action.
  • Pressable things scale to 0.97 on press. Popovers grow from their trigger.
  • Skeletons shaped like the content that is loading.
  • Radix-based shadcn primitives for anything with focus or keyboard behaviour.
  • h-dvh, safe-area insets for fixed controls, tabular-nums for counts.
  • Check every screen with long titles, 500 cards and an empty account.
Don't
  • Gradients, glows, glass panels, purple-to-blue anything.
  • Sparkle icons for AI. Stash is the AI's face.
  • "Unlock", "supercharge", "seamless", "revolutionize", emoji in UI copy.
  • Animating keyboard actions, the command palette, or tool switches.
  • Animating width, height, top or left. Transform and opacity only.
  • Entrance animations from scale(0) or with ease-in.
  • Fake testimonials, fake logos, fake user counts on the landing page.
  • Mixing icon families or stroke widths.
